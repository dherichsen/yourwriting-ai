ABOUT

We put the model in the building.

Crucible is forty-one people who think a company's AI should live where its data does. We install it, test it, tune it and grade it. The box is yours.

The best model in the world is the wrong one if your contracts have to leave to use it.

Crucible started in 2024 with three people who had spent a year on the wrong side of the same problem. One ran IT for a hospital group and had turned down every AI vendor because none of them could explain where the records went. One built models and was tired of watching good open ones sit unused because nobody would carry them into an office. One was the sysadmin who kept getting the security questionnaire.

So we did the unfashionable thing. We bought a workstation, put an open model on it, drove it to a clinic in Denver and plugged it in. The doctors asked it about their own notes, on their own network, and the answers came back in forty milliseconds. Nobody had to sign anything.

Everything since has been that afternoon, done properly: a guard so the frontier models can be used without the records leaving, a scorecard so the pick is evidence rather than taste, a grading floor so every answer gets read by a person, and a field team that installs a box in a morning. Four hundred and twelve buildings later, the job is the same.

What we hold to.

Local first.

The default answer comes from a box you own, in a building you control. The cloud is a per-task exception, and it goes out through a guard.

Measured, not promised.

Every model is scored on your tasks before it is picked, and every answer is graded after. We show the number before we ask for a decision.

You can leave.

Open weights, a standard index, your hardware. Support is the only thing you rent, and everything keeps working the day you stop.

People grade the work.

Overnight, humans read the answers. The model learns from the misses they mark, not from praise, and you get the report in the morning.
