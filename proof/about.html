ABOUT

We started with the voice. Then came the gate.

yourwriting.ai is forty-one people who put a tuned writing model in your building — and then built the thing that stops it making anything up.

A model that sounds exactly like your firm is the most dangerous place for a made-up number.

We started in 2024 doing one thing: taking an open model, putting it on a workstation in somebody's office, and tuning it on the letters that office had already sent. It worked. Drafts came back sounding like the practice rather than like a chatbot, and people stopped rewriting them.

In March 2025 one of those tuned models wrote a paragraph for a law firm citing a case number that did not exist. It was well-argued, correctly formatted, and in the partner's own cadence — which is precisely why it nearly went out. Nobody had asked the model to be honest, and nothing in the stack had checked it.

We spent the next six months building the gate: split the draft into claims, look each one up in the customer's own files, repair it or hold it, and attach a receipt. Every box we had already installed got it that September. Four hundred and twelve buildings later, the tune is what makes people use it and the gate is what lets them trust it.

What we hold to.

Tuned beats prompted.

A page of instructions in front of a rented model is a costume. A few thousand of your own letters change what the model is, and it holds up on the ten thousandth draft as well as the first.

A claim without a line is a guess.

Fluent text is not evidence. If the gate cannot put a file and a line against a claim, it does not go out — however confident, well-written and plausible it sounds.

People grade the writing.

Overnight, humans read the drafts and mark the misses. That is where the next adapter and the next gate rule come from, and it is the part nobody can automate away yet.

You can leave.

Open weights, your examples, your adapter, your source list, your logs — all in standard formats on hardware you own. Support is the only thing you rent.
