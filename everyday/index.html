Talk to us

Reasonable questions.

How is this different from asking a chatbot to write the email?

A chatbot writes the average of everyone. This is tuned on one archive — yours — so it comes back with your sentence lengths, your openings and the words you actually use. The difference shows up in how much you edit before sending, which is the only number that matters.

How much of my writing does it need?

About twenty thousand words, which for most people is a few months of sent mail. It's usable at that point and stops being noticeable somewhere around a hundred pieces.

Does it read my whole inbox?

Only what you sent. Incoming mail is used as context for the thread you're replying to and is never kept or learned from, because other people's writing is not yours to train on.

What if it invents something?

It won't fill a gap with a plausible number. If a claim isn't in the thread or a file you gave it, it leaves a blank and says what's missing — a blank is cheaper than a correction.

Can I turn it off for certain people?

Yes, per contact and per thread. Some letters you should write yourself, and the product is better for admitting that.

What happens if I cancel?

Export the voiceprint and the archive on the way out. Deleting the account deletes the tuned model the same day; there is no copy kept for anything.
