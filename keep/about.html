ABOUT

A small company that puts AI in small companies.

Keep is nine people in Tucson. We think your AI should live where your files do: in your building, under a desk, on your side. We bring it, set it up and keep it learning.

The best chatbot in the world is the wrong one if your customer files have to leave to use it.

Keep started in 2025 with one person and one problem. Sam ran IT for a twelve-person dental group and had turned down every AI vendor that walked in, because none of them could say, in plain words, where the patient files went. The doctors wanted the help. The answer to "where does it go" was always a shrug and a PDF.

So Sam did the unfashionable thing: bought a workstation, put an open model on it, and slid it under the front-desk counter. The staff asked it about their own schedules and their own notes, on their own network, and the answers came back in forty milliseconds. Nobody had to sign anything.

Everything since has been that afternoon, done properly: a guard so the big cloud models can be used without the files leaving, a scorecard so the pick is evidence rather than a sales pitch, a night shift of real people reading the answers, and a van. Twelve hundred and forty businesses later, the job is the same.

What we hold to.

Your building, not the cloud.

The default answer comes from a box you can point at. The cloud is a per-question exception, and it goes out through a guard.

Tested, not promised.

Every model is scored on work like yours before we pick one, and every answer is graded after. You see the numbers first.

You can leave.

Open models, your files in a normal format, hardware you can unplug. The monthly fee is the only thing you rent.

People read the answers.

Overnight, real people grade what the box said. It learns from the misses they mark; you get the report with your coffee.
