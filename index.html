INSIDE THE MACHINE

What you're
actually buying.

Rent the same model as your competitors and you'll ship the same answers. The layers underneath are where the difference is made — and the top one is a receipt.

  1. The receipt

    What your reviewer touches: every claim, the file and line it came from, what was repaired, what's held.

  2. The gate

    Splits the draft into claims and checks each one against your files. Supported, repaired or held — no third answer.

  3. Evaluation

    Planted errors, poisoned sources, a blind judge. Proof it works before your clients find out it doesn't.

  4. Human review

    Your people grade the misses. Every one becomes a training example or a new gate rule.

  5. The tune

    A thin adapter learned from the letters you already send, so drafts land in your voice.

  6. Your files

    Invoices, contracts, policies, sent mail. The only truth the gate accepts — and the part money can't shortcut.

HOW IT LEARNS YOUR VOICE

Five steps, four weeks, one job at a time.

Fine-tuning is the whole product here, not an add-on. The box does the work; your people do the judging.

01 / 05GATHER
  1. 01 · GATHER

    The writing you already send.

    Sent mail, proposals, quotes, reports, the replies your best people write on a good day. We pull them from the systems they already live in onto the box in your building. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  2. 02 · REFINE

    Deduplicated, redacted, graded by your people.

    Near-duplicates drop out. Names, account numbers and anything personal are hashed before a single example is used. Then two or three of your people grade a sample, so the model learns from letters you would sign.

  3. 03 · TUNE

    A thin adapter learns your voice. The base model stays intact.

    About 0.4% of the weights learn your formats, your vocabulary and your habits over a few hours on the box. One adapter per team, and any of them rolls back in a click.

  4. 04 · PROVE

    Graded blind, against the model you'd otherwise rent.

    Held-out letters nobody trained on become the exam. Base model, your tuned model and the best guarded frontier model are scored side by side by the same people, blind. You see the number before anything ships.

  5. 05 · SHIP

    Live on the box, with the gate in front of it.

    From that moment every draft goes through the gate before a person sees it, and the overnight grading keeps feeding the next version: every miss your people mark becomes next week's training example.

WHERE WE'LL TELL YOU NO

What the gate will not do for you.

  • It can't check what you don't have.

    The gate proves a claim against your files. If the answer lives in somebody's head, or in a system we were not given, the claim comes back unverified and the draft says so. That is the gate working, and it is the most common reason a first week is noisy.

  • It won't make a weak model clever.

    Tuning teaches habits, not intelligence. A tuned open model beats a frontier one on your letters because it has seen your letters. Ask it something genuinely hard and unfamiliar and it is the base model again — that is what the guarded frontier path is for.

  • It isn't a compliance sign-off.

    Every claim traced and logged is evidence, not approval. The regulated letters still get a human name against them, and the gate's log exists to make that review quick rather than to replace it.
