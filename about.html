ABOUT

We started with the voice. Then came the gate.

yourwriting.ai is a small team that tunes a writing model on what your firm has already sent — and built the thing that stops it making anything up.

A model that sounds exactly like your firm is the most dangerous place for a made-up number.

We started inside a studio that writes for firms in regulated work: clinics, treatment centres, law practices. The job was to make machine drafts read like the people who sign them, and we measured it the hard way — nineteen rounds of tuning and prompting through 2026, graded blind against human-written pages, with every idea that failed written down next to the ones that worked.

The voice turned out to be the easier half. A draft that sounds exactly like the partner is the one nobody re-reads — and that is where a wrong figure, a date from a superseded contract or a case that does not exist walks straight out the door in the firm's own cadence.

So we built the gate: split the draft into claims, look each one up in the firm's own files, repair it or hold it, and sign a receipt that says what was checked. It has a written evaluation — planted errors, poisoned sources, a second model as blind judge — and the numbers on the home page come from it, not from a slide.

What we hold to.

Tuned beats prompted.

A page of instructions in front of a rented model is a costume. A few thousand of your own letters change what the model is, and it holds up on the ten thousandth draft as well as the first.

A claim without a line is a guess.

Fluent text is not evidence. If the gate cannot put a file and a line against a claim, it does not go out — however confident, well-written and plausible it sounds.

People grade the writing.

Humans read the drafts and mark the misses. That is where the next adapter and the next gate rule come from, and it is the part nobody can automate away yet.

You can leave.

Open weights, your examples, your adapter, your source list, your logs — all in standard formats on hardware you own. Support is the only thing you rent.

How we got here.

  1. 2026The measurement

    Nineteen rounds of making machine drafts read like the firm, graded blind against human-written pages. Six ideas kept, eight thrown out, all of it on the record.

  2. SEP 2026The call

    The fine-tuning is a feature. The proof that the writing was checked is the product. yourwriting.ai gets its brief.

  3. OCT 2026The gate

    Version 0.3: claims, sources, verdicts, receipts. A hundred and sixty evaluation runs with planted and poisoned errors, judged blind.

  4. NOWFirst pilots

    One kind of letter, four weeks, a fixed fee. Firms that bill for words and carry liability for them go first.
